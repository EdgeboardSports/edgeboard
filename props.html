How the model and checks work

Shots v1 uses up to 40 prior regular-season games: equal weight to the last 10 and full sample shot averages, with small adjustments for venue, past games against this opponent, and recent ice time. It uses a Poisson count distribution or a negative-binomial distribution when past shot counts are more variable.

Each test predicts a historical game using only earlier dates. We test up to 40 games after a minimum of 20 training games, at today's selected threshold. Brier loss measures forecast error (lower is better); the baseline is a smoothed historical hit rate. Calibration bins compare predicted over rates with actual outcomes.

Exploratory checks require 30 held-out games, Brier loss no worse than baseline, positive expected value even when expected shots change by ±15%, a quote updated within 15 minutes, a future start, a verified team/venue match, and an official current-roster check no older than 10 minutes. Roster membership does not confirm tonight’s lineup. The ±15% range is a sensitivity check, not a confidence interval. These checks do not establish statistical significance or profitability.

No historical sportsbook prices are used; this is a probability backtest, not a return-on-investment backtest. Injury and role-change reports are screening checks; published scratches block candidates. ESPN goalie statuses are shown as projected, confirmed or unknown. Empty scratch reports do not confirm a full lineup. These context signals do not adjust probability estimates. No automatic probability calibration has been fitted.
