Model assumptions, tests & settlement

Independent regulation goal counts use recent offense, opponent goals allowed and venue splits, with a fixed 2.8-goal prior. A regulation tie is split 50/50 for overtime/shootout modeling; that split is stress-tested at 40/60. Starting goalies, injuries, lineups, empty-net effects and correlated scoring are not explicitly modeled.

Up to 100 held-out league games are predicted using only earlier game dates. Each team needs 20 prior regular-season games. The benchmark is equal-strength Poisson goals (2.8 each); it is not the sportsbook market. Lower Brier loss is better. Parameters are fixed, not optimized on this displayed test.

Research candidates require 100 held-out tests, error no worse than baseline, positive expected value under ±15% goal-rate and 40–60% overtime-share sensitivity, fresh quotes, a verified pregame matchup and a complete team archive under 48 hours old. These are exploratory checks, not statistical significance or proof of profitability.

Moneyline and standard puck line use the official final score including overtime/shootout (one shootout winner goal). Regulation three-way uses home win/draw/away win after 60 minutes. Missing regulation-end metadata stays ungraded; it is never inferred from an overtime winner alone. Book-specific rules may differ from these paper rules.

Estimated return is per unit staked, accounting for pushes. Historical sportsbook prices are not available for the backtest; forward paper records preserve actual quoted prices before play.
