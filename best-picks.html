Coverage, exclusions & scan times

Props currently ranks shots on goal only. Goals, assists and points are available in the matchup lab but do not have validated ranking models. Only selected upcoming games and provider-offered markets are scanned. Missing feeds or player histories reduce coverage; they never become invented picks.

What makes a candidate?

Both models require positive estimated value, sensitivity checks, fresh quotes, verified pregame context and held-out forecast performance no worse than their baseline. Shots requires 30 test games; game bets requires 100 and a complete recent team archive. Passing these exploratory checks does not establish profitability.
